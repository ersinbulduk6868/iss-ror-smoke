# ISS Battle Video Visual Acceptance vNext

Status: LOCKED for the current Blender battle-video iteration.

These gates are additive. After each rendered video is reviewed, newly observed shortcomings become new acceptance items. Iteration continues until the desired visual result is reached. Existing frozen 9-service architecture and previously locked service contracts remain unchanged.

## A1 — Two-sided approach
- Bugatti and bulldozer must both move under the collision setup.
- They must approach each other rather than one object remaining passive.

## A2 — Head-on center impact
- The vehicles must collide approximately head-on near the center of the scene.
- The impact must be clearly readable in the rendered video.

## A3 — Bulldozer active motion and response
- Bulldozer must have its own pre-impact motion.
- It must show a visible post-impact physical response while still reflecting its much greater mass.

## A4 — Readable collision effect
- Bugatti front-impact effect must be visible and readable.
- Lighting, camera and render quality must not hide the deformation/impact outcome.

## A5 — Breakage / fragmentation / debris
- The impact outcome must include visible breakage, detached fragments, debris, or equivalent persistent impact evidence.
- Any damage/debris used for this test must be triggered from the actually observed impact event, not pre-played before collision.

## A6 — Improved image quality
- Previous low-quality test look is not accepted.
- Render must materially improve resolution, shading, materials, shadows and edge quality.

## A7 — No flat test camera
- A single static technical-test camera is insufficient.
- The video must use purposeful visual storytelling.

## A8 — Aftermath camera approach
- After impact, the camera must move closer and show the result.
- Final vehicle positions, damage and debris must be readable.

## A9 — Story-driven visual angles
- Use a clear approach shot, an impact-focused shot and an aftermath shot.
- Camera changes must support the battle event rather than distract from it.

## A10 — Iterative acceptance expansion
- After each new rendered video is reviewed, newly observed shortcomings become additional locked acceptance items.
- Improvements continue until the user accepts the target result.

## A11 — Environment and lighting
- The scene must not be too dark.
- Both vehicles and the impact area must remain clearly visible before, during and after collision.
- Use sufficient key/fill/environment light, readable shadows and a brighter environment.

## Current evaluation dimensions
1. Physics behavior
2. Image/render quality
3. Collision-effect readability
4. Story/cinematic presentation

This acceptance set does not by itself declare production acceptance. It is the locked target for the next visual collision test.
